🧭 BCA Student Career Roadmap

Step 1 – Learn Programming

Start with C, C++, Java or Python. Understand programming fundamentals and problem-solving.

Step 2 – Build Technical Skills

Learn HTML, CSS, JavaScript, DBMS, SQL, Data Structures and other important subjects.

Step 3 – Work on Projects

Create websites, applications and mini projects. Upload your work to GitHub.

Step 4 – Build Your Resume

Prepare a professional resume and showcase your projects and technical skills.

Step 5 – Internship and Jobs

Apply for internships, participate in interviews and explore entry-level opportunities.

Step 6 – Higher Education

Explore MCA, MSc Computer Science and other relevant postgraduate programs.
